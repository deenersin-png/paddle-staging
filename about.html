Paddle: Find your run in seconds

Built by Deen Ersin, a SEPTA operator at Callowhill, for operators. Free for every coworker.

Type your run number and get your scorecard instantly:

  • Pull-out and pull-in times
  • Every route and route change
  • Scheduled hours and pay
  • Swing runs with the gap and both pieces clearly shown

Hunting for a specific route? Filter by route (21-42, 65, whatever you want) and see every run that covers it across Weekday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Found a run you like? Tap PADDLE to open the official PDF right to your page.

Please note: This is an unofficial tool for orientation only. It may contain errors. Always confirm your run details against the official SEPTA files.

Questions? Contact Deen at dersin@septa.org.

Works on your phone. No download, no cost.
